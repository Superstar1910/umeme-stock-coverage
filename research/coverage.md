# Umeme: a recovery and distribution framework
**Research architecture release, 13 October 2026. Historical analysis; no current investment recommendation.**

## Investment question
The project's prior analysis asks how to value the shareholder interest after concession exit. Separate the operating utility's historical economics from cash retained, recovery rights, debts and disputed offsets. Published Substack articles frame the uncertain recovery as optionality. This is an economic analogy: ordinary shares do not acquire a contractual strike or expiry merely because a payoff is asymmetric.

## What determines remaining value?
1. Unrestricted cash at a verified balance-sheet cutoff.
2. Incremental recoveries, enforceability, collection date and FX.
3. Debt, recognised and disputed liabilities, tax, legal costs and reserves.
4. Distributions already paid and eligibility for future payments.
5. Share count and trading liquidity at the relevant date.

## Scenarios and decision rules
Use zero, partial and full recovery cases, each with a corresponding liability settlement and timing. Probabilities must sum to one. Show sensitivity to delay, discount rate, FX and costs. A price comparison needs a verified quote and ex-dividend status.

Evidence that changes the thesis includes company accounts, formal arbitration disclosures, liability reconciliation, actual distributions and corporate-action notices. A claim announcement changes neither cash nor shareholder proceeds until recognition and receipt are established.
