# Publication and refresh rules
Owner: Kenneth S. Legesi. Initial release: 13 October 2026.

Preserve Substack originals through canonical links. Do not label reconstructed chat material as a verbatim article. Every numerical update needs dated sources, cutoff, units, outstanding shares, cash reconciliation and an assumptions version. Source assertions, analyst estimates and reported facts must remain distinct.

A new price target requires verified market/ex-dividend information and the latest cash/liability position. Review legal-status statements against formal disclosures. Store allegations as attributed assertions. Use scenario probabilities as estimates, not facts.

Review weekly and after results, arbitration announcements, liability reconciliation or distributions. Automation can validate and remind; it must not automatically upgrade evidence or issue recommendations. Keep corrections in the ledger and prior snapshots in version history.

No private correspondence, investor records, privileged advice or personal holdings are included. Disclosures of author positions/conflicts remain unprovided. Third-party documents should be linked unless redistribution rights are established. Complete project migration is still open.
